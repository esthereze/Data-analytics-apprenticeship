## DAY 3 ASSIGNMENT

Imagine you're the analyst for a supermarket. The owner gives you this business problem:

***"Our revenue has fallen by 15% this month. I want to know why."***

### Part A — Choose KPIs

Choose 8 KPIs you would investigate. For each one, explain:

KPI:
- Why I chose it:
- What it could tell me:

Example:
KPI: Number of transactions
- Why: I want to know whether fewer customers are purchasing.
- What it could tell me: If transactions dropped significantly, declining customer traffic could be contributing to the revenue decline.
**Do not copy that one. Make your own.**

### Part B — Choose dimensions

Choose at least 6 dimensions you would use to break down the data.

For example:
Product category
Month
**But I want you to think of six yourself.**

Explain why each matters.
